# 🏟️ Genius Stadium

Fast, short brain games for a curious 5th grader: **maps and flags, chess puzzles, soccer trivia, and wild facts**. You earn XP, levels, badges, and daily streaks.

**Play:** https://nancivelascoo-del.github.io/genius-stadium/ (on iPad: Share → Add to Home Screen; it works offline)

- 🌍 **Geo Arena:** tap countries or US states on the map against the clock. Also Flag Frenzy, Capital Clash, State Capitals, and Explore.
- ♟️ **Chess Lab:** bite-size lessons (back-rank mate, forks, pins, skewers, discovered attacks, famous mates), a Puzzle Ladder, and a 3-minute Tactic Sprint.
- ⚽ **Soccer Stadium:** a penalty shootout where right answers let you shoot (fast answers beat the keeper), plus soccer trivia.
- 💡 **Fact Lab:** Fact of the Day, a 60-second Fact or Fib blitz, a Quick Quiz, and a Fact Vault to fill.
- 📅 **Daily Challenge:** 5 mixed questions plus a puzzle of the day to keep your streak going.

There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. Progress is saved on the device, and the app loads nothing from other websites.
